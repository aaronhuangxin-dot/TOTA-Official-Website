香港智造的健康坚持A Hong Kong standard for everyday health

TOTA 是一家扎根香港、专注膳食营养补充领域的本土健康企业，秉持「香港智造，品质为本」的核心理念，深耕骨关节养护与日常营养赛道。TOTA is a Hong Kong–rooted nutrition company built on the principle of "Made in Hong Kong, Quality First," focused on joint care and everyday nutrition.

TOTA 秉持「香港智造，品质为本」的核心理念，深耕骨关节养护与日常营养赛道，产品覆盖氨糖软骨素系列、维生素软糖系列两大核心品类。Guided by the principle of "Made in Hong Kong, Quality First," TOTA is focused on joint care and everyday nutrition, with products spanning two core lines: glucosamine chondroitin and daily vitamin gummies.

公司坚持全链路香港本土生产，生产基地严格遵循 GMP 良好生产规范，执行香港食物环境卫生署监管标准，从原料甄选、配方研发到成品出厂，全流程闭环质控，确保每一款产品均符合香港食品安全与品质要求。We hold to end-to-end Hong Kong production: our facilities follow GMP good manufacturing practice and Hong Kong FEHD regulatory standards, with closed-loop quality control from raw material selection through formula development to finished product — ensuring every item meets Hong Kong's food safety and quality requirements.

依托成熟的本土供应链体系，我们致力于打造高信赖度的香港制造健康产品，让每一位消费者都能安心选择。Backed by a mature local supply chain, we aim to build trustworthy, Hong Kong–made health products that customers can choose with confidence.
