氨糖软骨素 · 8大营养因数Glucosamine Chondroitin — Eight Nutrients in One Formula

氨基葡萄糖硫酸钾盐和硫酸软骨素，是人体软骨细胞的重要营养素；特有的 MSM，是人体胶原蛋白合成的必要物质。特别添加姜黄提取物与透明质酸钠，双效强化，缓解关节疲劳，促进受损骨骼肌修复。 Glucosamine sulfate and chondroitin sulfate are key nutrients for cartilage cells, while MSM supports the body's own collagen synthesis. Added turmeric extract and sodium hyaluronate ease joint fatigue and support recovery.

适合人群：特别适合中老年关节退化、健身、登山、跑步等关节负荷过重的人群，也适合久坐不动的上班族。 Best for: people with age-related joint wear, athletes, hikers and runners under heavy joint load, and office workers who sit for long hours.
